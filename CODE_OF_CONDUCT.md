# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We as members, contributors, and maintainers pledge to make participation in this project a
harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible
disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual
identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that contributes to a positive environment:

- Being respectful and constructive when giving and receiving feedback.
- Accepting responsibility for mistakes and learning from them.
- Focusing on what is best for the community.

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks.
- Publishing others' private information without their explicit permission.
- Any conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behaviour may be reported to the project
maintainer by opening a GitHub issue marked as a conduct report, or privately through a GitHub security
advisory. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly. The maintainer is
obliged to respect the privacy and security of the reporter.

Maintainers will decide on an appropriate response, which may range from a private warning to a
temporary or permanent ban from the project spaces.
